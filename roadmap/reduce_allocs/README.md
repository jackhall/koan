# Reduce allocations

Cut the execute path's measured allocation traffic. The recorded baselines in
[audit/README.md](../../audit/README.md) put a number on every step and every dispatch —
206 allocations per tail-loop step, ≈55 per operator dispatch — and
`tests/allocation_baseline.rs` holds the shapes to a bound, so each item here removes a
named term from a measured total rather than an assumed one. A dhat profile of the audit
shapes (2026-08-18, differencing runs at two sizes to isolate the scaling term) attributes
the per-step cost across six near-equal subsystems; the items below each own one.

## Unplanned work

Scaling terms the profile attributes but no item yet owns. Each entry names the measured
share and its top sites; promoting one to a work item means writing its file with
acceptance criteria and wiring it into the dependency graph.

- **Outcome and obligation boxing** — ≈30 of the 206 allocations per tail-loop step: a
  fresh boxed continuation per step through `ignore_results`
  ([src/machine/execute/outcome.rs](../../src/machine/execute/outcome.rs), 10/step),
  `with_obligation` and `ReturnObligation::duplicate`
  ([src/machine/execute/obligation.rs](../../src/machine/execute/obligation.rs), 8/step
  each), plus `all_or_first_error` and `short_circuit`.
- **Frame and scope bookkeeping** — ≈28 of the 206 allocations per tail-loop step:
  `Scope::adopt_carried` ([src/machine/core/scope/reach.rs](../../src/machine/core/scope/reach.rs),
  9/step), `build_frame_child_witnessed` and `CallFrame::new`
  ([src/machine/core/arena/frame.rs](../../src/machine/core/arena/frame.rs), 6+4/step),
  `LexicalFrame::push`, and `SlotFrame` construction.
