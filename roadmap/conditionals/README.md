# Conditionals

Branching on a value's type, on a union's variant, on a value, and on whether a
computation faulted. `MATCH` selects the most specific arm whose guard admits
its scrutinee, as dispatch selects a candidate; `TRY` selects an arm by a
fault's kind the same way, and `CATCH` turns a fault into a `Result`. A fault
carries structured fields and the trace of calls it passed through, so an
uncaught one prints where it came from. What it buys the language: its whole
branching surface under one selection rule, rather than a mechanism apiece for
types, variants and errors.

The layers underneath are [dispatch](../../src/dispatch/README.md) and the
[program](../../src/program/README.md#faults-and-output).
