# Traced layers at load

A keyed body's facts under each binding the load can name, laid by the load.

**Problem.** A call whose binding [the trace](the-trace.md) names still seeks
a settlement at run: the drain hashes the content at each keyed name, probes
the arena, and on a miss runs the walk there and then, synchronously, and the
frame is born under the new arena node
([steps and the drain](../../src/build/README.md#steps-and-the-drain)). The
load held everything that walk reads — the body, the parent's facts and the
binding — and ran nothing.

**Acceptance criteria.**

- A keyed call whose every call site passes content the trace names seeks
  nothing: the load lays the body's facts under each traced binding as a
  layer in program storage ([one layer](one-layer.md)), indexed per call site
  as a route is, and the frame reads them through its door as it reads a
  settlement's today.
- A call site the trace marks untraced seeks a settlement as today; both
  paths run side by side, and the arena's books are unchanged.
- The property laws hold unchanged: a load-time layer equals a restart with
  the binding declared, as a settlement does
  ([properties](../../src/build/README.md#properties)).
- The invariant mint sites of a caching body under a traced binding rest
  their types in the frozen load, laid by the load beside the layer; under an
  untraced one they rest where a settlement rests them today
  ([where each body interns](../../design/type-tiers.md#where-each-body-interns)).
- The count [the trace](the-trace.md) reports is matched by the settlements
  a run no longer keys: every traced site keys none.

**Directions.**

- *The walk — decided.* The load runs the one walk rooted at the body over a
  `Layer` on the base, as a settlement's does, once per traced binding, after
  the base walk and before the folds that read the layers.
- *Where a traced refusal lands — open.* The load refuses, as it refuses
  code no run reaches, since the binding is the text's; or the layer keeps
  its refusal and the call faults, as a settlement does today
  ([refusal](../../src/build/README.md#refusal)).
- *A site traced to a set — open.* A layer per member of the set, each
  indexed from the site beside the content that selects it; or the site left
  untraced. Sized by the trace's count.

## Dependencies

**Requires:**

- [One layer, wherever laid](one-layer.md) — the form the load lays.
- [The trace](the-trace.md) — the bindings it lays under.

**Unblocks:**

- [Settlements retired](settlements-retired.md) — what the load lays leaves
  the arena only the untraced remainder.
