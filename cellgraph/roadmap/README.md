## Unplanned work

Known gaps with no item behind them yet — recorded so they are not rediscovered
as surprises, not scheduled.

- **Group sealing.** Seal-time absorption is per region: it fires on a count of
  1, so a region held only *within* a dying subtree carries a count above 1 at
  each individual seal and stays its own record, leaving the subtree as a chain
  of records ([design/liveness-matrix.md § Locality
  tactics](../design/liveness-matrix.md#locality-tactics)). Sealing a delimited
  group as one record would collapse the chain and, the larger payoff, erase its
  internal holds entirely — only the group's boundary reach would survive the
  seal. Delimiting the group is the design work, and nothing in the substrate
  expresses one today.
